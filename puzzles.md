## The Massachusetts Mysteries
I’m designing [city scavenger puzzle hunts](https://themassachusettsmysteries.com/) that run through different neighborhoods of Boston/Cambridge/Somerville! They are slightly easier than PuzzledPint and targeted towards a more general audience. Events will run on a somewhat-monthly basis, though we’ll take a break during the winter – the best way to stay up to date is to follow our Humanitix organizer profile.

## PuzzledPint

[Puzzled Pint]((puzzledpint.org)) is a casual, social puzzle solving event which happens at bars/pubs on the second Tuesday of every month in multiple cities around the world (35+ cities and counting!). These puzzle sets are designed to take between 1-3 hours, depending on player experience levels.

## Virtual Escape Rooms

These virtual “escape rooms” are available through Google Forms and are best played in groups of 2-3 people who are working together either remotely or in person.

- [magfest escape room](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdHYJU3Qi4YaWOLRGDAHVtWkTVDaiEykpxvbzbGlZRp3SYdPg/viewform) – Made in 2021 when one of my favorite gaming conventions, MAGFest, was cancelled due to the pandemic.
