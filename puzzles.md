## [The Massachusetts Mysteries](https://themassachusettsmysteries.com/)
I’m designing city scavenger puzzle hunts that run through different neighborhoods of Boston/Cambridge/Somerville! They are slightly easier than PuzzledPint and targeted towards a more general audience. Events will run on a somewhat-monthly basis, though we’ll take a break during the winter – the best way to stay up to date is to follow our Humanitix organizer profile.

## [PuzzledPint](puzzledpint.org)

Puzzled Pint is a casual, social puzzle solving event which happens at bars/pubs on the second Tuesday of every month in multiple cities around the world (35+ cities and counting!). These puzzle sets are designed to take between 1-3 hours, depending on player experience levels.
